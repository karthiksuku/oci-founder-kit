# Startup Growth Engine: fit check and application draft

Criteria and terms summarised from Oracle's program page and terms, checked
2026-10-21. Dates and sources: `../../founder-value/references/facts.md`.
Re-check them before relying on this file.

## Fit check

| Published criterion | Ask | Evidence to collect |
|---|---|---|
| Legally registered technology company building digital products or platforms | Incorporated? Where? | Country of incorporation; check it is a designated country on the live page |
| Proprietary product validated with customers or active pilots | Paying customers or pilots today? | Count, names only if the founder can share, a usage metric |
| Software, data, cloud-native or AI product | What is the product? | One-sentence description |
| Clear potential to scale | Growth so far and plan | Monthly growth, pipeline, the next milestone |
| A defined workload to build or migrate to OCI | Which workload, how big? | The `founder-value` scenario: services and monthly consumption at list price |
| Relevant enterprise or industry use cases | Who buys it? | Target industries and buyer roles |
| Leadership and technical resources committed | Who will do the work? | Named owner and hours available |
| Not a public entity; no sanctions, financial-crime or reputational risk; no unpaid Oracle debts | Any of these? | Stop if yes |
| Existing OCI customer | Already on OCI? | If yes, a substantial growth case is required |

Sectors with extra due diligence: financial services, crypto-asset
platforms, online betting or gaming, prediction markets and digital stock
exchanges. Warn the founder the review may take longer.

**Verdict:** *Apply now* when every row has evidence; *Apply after* when one
or two rows have a gap the founder can close in weeks; *Not a fit* at any
exclusion or when there is no product validation yet.

## Application draft

The application asks about the company, product, customers and growth plans.
Draft each answer from evidence and mark gaps as `[GAP: ...]` rather than
filling them with plausible text.

```markdown
## Company
<legal name, country, founded, team size, funding stage>

## Product
<what it does, for whom, why now; one paragraph>

## Validation
<customers or pilots, a usage or revenue metric, a quote only if the founder has permission>

## Workload on OCI
<services, monthly consumption at list price from the value model with the price list date,
growth over 12 months, what credits would fund (migration rehearsal, load tests, AI evaluation)>

## Growth plan
<next 12 months: customers, markets, the milestone credits and support would accelerate>

## Go-to-market fit
<target industries and buyers; why Oracle's customers are relevant; readiness for Marketplace>
```

What credits should fund matters: a reviewer can see a credit request tied to
specific work, such as migration rehearsal or AI evaluation, that leads to
lasting consumption.

## After an award

- Record the amount, the date credits became available and the 12-month
  expiry in the scenario's `credits` block, as `stated`.
- Plan work that uses credits before they expire.
- Budget for the cliff: the month after credits end, the tenancy pays list
  price.
